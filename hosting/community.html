earlyworld community

Community rules

Share only content you created or have permission to share. No harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, exploitation, impersonation, or disclosure of someone’s private information.

Posts, written reviews, comments, and profile changes publish without advance moderator approval. Photos must finish processing first. You can report content, block listeners, and delete your own posts. This release supports text and photos, not video uploads.

Privacy and access

Community content is visible to signed-in listeners. Bookmarks, reports, and stored eligibility information are private. Eligibility information is not displayed on profiles. Publishing requires completed onboarding, and written reviews also require a verified email and confirmed community eligibility. US residence and age are self-declared, and posts and comments do not have those extra requirements.

Firebase and Google Cloud process account details, submitted content, uploaded photos, and app diagnostics. The app uses Firebase Analytics for usage events and Crashlytics for error reports. Catalog artwork and external links may contact their source services. There are no direct messages, contact discovery, location collection for community features, or targeted advertising.

Private photo links expire after five minutes. Abandoned server drafts become eligible for cleanup after seven days. Account deletion removes the account, profile, authored posts, uploaded media, reviews, ratings, saves, and social connections through background cleanup. Previously issued media links may work until expiry. Minimal deletion and security records can remain; provider logs and backups follow their configured retention rather than immediate deletion. Catalog metadata and other listeners’ independent content remain.

These descriptions reflect the current implementation. Public-launch privacy and age-policy review remains outstanding; this page does not claim that review is complete.

Reports and support

Report posts, comments, reviews, or profiles using their report action. Manage blocked listeners in Settings. For rights complaints, send the content link and a description to earlyworldofficial@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Account deletion

Use Settings → Delete account, or request account deletion on the web.
