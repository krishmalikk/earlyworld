earlyworld community

Social features are in internal testing. Public availability requires completion of our moderation and privacy review.

Community rules

Share only content you created or have permission to share, including music in videos. No harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, exploitation, impersonation, or disclosure of private information. Posts, videos, written reviews, comments, and profile changes require moderator approval.

Privacy and access

The initial community is for US users aged 13 and older. Viewing requires sign-in. Eligibility information, drafts, reports, and bookmarks are private. We store an age band rather than a birth date. These features do not collect location or contacts, enable direct messages, or use targeted advertising. Firebase and Google Cloud store and process submitted content. Authorized media links expire after five minutes.

Abandoned drafts expire after seven days. Deleting a post hides it immediately, while queued cleanup removes its media. Account deletion is available under Settings → Delete account and removes authored content, uploads, reviews, saves, and the profile. Already issued links may work until expiry.

Reports and support

Use the report action on posts, comments, reviews, and profiles. You can block listeners and manage blocks in Settings → Blocked listeners. For rights complaints or assistance, email earlyworldofficial@gmail.com with the post link, a description of the issue, and contact details. Do not send sensitive personal information.
