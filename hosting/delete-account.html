Delete your earlyworld account

You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the app.

Request deletion by email

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, write to earlyworldofficial@gmail.com with the subject “earlyworld account deletion request”. Opening the email draft does not submit a request: you must send it.

Include your earlyworld username and account email so support can locate your account. Send from that address when possible. If you used Apple Hide My Email, include your username and Apple relay email if available. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents, or payment information.

Support must verify account ownership before arranging deletion. An email address or username alone is not proof of ownership. Support will reply with the next steps; submitting an email does not immediately delete your account.

Delete inside the app

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account. Read the explanation and confirm. A recent sign-in is required. Apple-linked accounts also authorize Apple access revocation on iPhone. Signing in again never submits deletion automatically.

What is removed

Deletion removes your sign-in account, profile, authored posts, uploaded photos, reviews, ratings, saves, private bookmarks, and social connections. Cleanup runs in the background after acceptance. Private media links already issued may work for up to five minutes.

Shared catalog metadata and other listeners’ independent content remain. Minimal deletion and security records can remain to prevent continued access and diagnose cleanup failures. Service-provider logs and backups follow their configured retention; this is not a promise of immediate removal from backups. Support correspondence is separate from the app account; mention it if your request includes that correspondence.

Deletion cannot be undone. Support will confirm completion after cleanup, rather than when a request is first received.
