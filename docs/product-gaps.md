# What would make earlyworld feel finished

Reviewed November 2, 2026 against the `production-implementation` working tree, by reading the code and walking the signed-in app in the iPhone 17 simulator. This is about the product feeling complete from the first screen to the last, not about launch blockers; those are in [launch-readiness.md](launch-readiness.md). Nothing here is a claim that the live app or a physical device was tested today.

The app already has a strong core: ratings, saves, favorites, matching, Rotation, Studio credits, posts, share cards, a diary, listener search, follow lists and an activity list. The gaps below are the places where a user hits a dead end, has to guess, or gets less feedback than they would in an app they trust.

Each item has a rough size: **S** under half a day, **M** one to two days, **L** more.

## What I'd do first

1. Artist search in onboarding, welcome card, and the feature explainers (section 1). These change how the first ten minutes feel.
2. Optimistic save/like, haptics and the shared toast (sections 3 and 7). These are what people mean by "it feels finished".
3. Report sheet and admin inbox (sections 4 and 5). Required before strangers join.
4. Profile overflow menu, photo removal and username note (section 6).
5. Skeletons and offline banner (section 7).

Everything else can follow the invited beta, where real usage will reorder the list.
