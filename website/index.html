FAQ

Good questions

Can I play music inside the app?

No — and that's on purpose. earlyworld links out to each track's real source so artists and producers get the play where it counts. There's no in-app player, no autoplay, and no listening telemetry.

What makes a save "rare"?

Rarity is based on how few people have saved a track. The fewer the saves, the more a shared save weighs in taste matching — so finding and saving something early is what connects you to the right people.

Where do producer credits come from?

Credits are enriched conservatively when a track is added, and any credit you enter as a user takes precedence. Missing credits stay blank rather than guessed.

How much does it cost?

earlyworld is launching with early access. There are no ads and no trackers — join the list below and we'll tell you the moment your spot is ready.

Which platforms are supported?

earlyworld is a native app for iOS and Android. It's not a web app — the experience is built for your phone.
